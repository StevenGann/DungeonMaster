## Special Abilities

### Unarmed Combat
*Your fists are deadly weapons.*

Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 damage instead of the normal 1d3. You are never considered unarmed.

### Flurry of Blows
*You can strike multiple times with blinding speed.*

Once per combat, you can make two additional unarmed attacks in a single turn. You take -1 to all attack rolls this turn.

### Stunning Strike
*You can disrupt an enemy's vital energy.*

When you hit with an unarmed attack, you can attempt to stun the target. The target must make a Physical + STRENGTH check (DC 14) or be stunned until the start of your next turn. A stunned creature cannot take actions. Usable 2 times per day.

### Slow Fall
*You can reduce falling damage through careful movement.*

When you fall, reduce the damage taken by 10. If this reduces the damage to 0 or less, you land on your feet.

### Unarmored Defense
*Your training allows you to dodge attacks without armor.*

While wearing no armor, your AC equals 10 + DEX bonus + MIND bonus (already calculated above).

## Backstory

Grimjaw was raised in the Iron Temple, a monastery built deep within the mountains where dwarven monks practice the Way of the Stone Fist. Unlike traditional dwarven warriors who rely on axes and heavy armor, the monks of the Iron Temple believe true strength comes from within. Grimjaw left the temple to test his skills in the wider world and prove that discipline of mind and body can overcome any obstacle. He seeks both worthy opponents and inner peace.
