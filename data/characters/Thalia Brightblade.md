## Special Abilities

### Favored Enemy: Goblins
*You have studied goblins extensively and know their weaknesses.*

Add +2 to attack rolls and damage rolls against goblinoid creatures (goblins, hobgoblins, bugbears).

### Track
*You can follow the trail of creatures through the wilderness.*

Add +4 to Survival checks when tracking creatures. You can identify the type and number of creatures, how long ago they passed, and their general condition.

### Two-Weapon Fighting
*You can fight with a weapon in each hand.*

When wielding a weapon in each hand, you can make an additional attack per turn at -2 to both attack rolls.

### Wild Empathy
*You can communicate with animals.*

You can attempt to calm or befriend wild animals by making a Communication + MIND check. The difficulty depends on the animal's temperament.

## Backstory

Thalia grew up on the edge of the Whispering Woods, the child of an elven hunter and a human herbalist. When a goblin warband destroyed her family's homestead, she swore to protect others from suffering the same fate. Now she wanders the frontier, offering her bow and tracking skills to those in need, always with an ear to the ground for news of goblin activity.
