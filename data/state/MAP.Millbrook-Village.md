## Atmosphere Notes

- **Smell:** Livestock, wood smoke, river mud
- **Sound:** Mill creek (now silent), market chatter, forge hammer
- **Streets:** Muddy, unpaved except Main Street
- **Mood:** Working folk, wary of strangers but warm to helpful outsiders
