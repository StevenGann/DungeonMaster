# The Greenvale Region

*A frontier land where civilization meets the wild*

---

## Overview

The Greenvale Region is a temperate frontier territory at the edge of the kingdom, where rolling farmland gives way to dense forests and misty hills. Once a prosperous mining area, the region fell into decline after the ore veins ran dry. Now it's a quiet backwater—but recent monster sightings and rumors of ancient ruins have drawn adventurers seeking fortune and glory.

---

## Geography

The region spans roughly 50 miles east to west and 40 miles north to south. The landscape transitions from cultivated farmland in the south to untamed wilderness in the north. The climate is temperate with mild summers, cold winters, and frequent rain.

### Elevation
- **South:** Low-lying farmland and gentle hills (200-400 ft)
- **Central:** Rolling terrain with scattered woodlands (400-800 ft)
- **North:** Rugged hills and dense forest (800-1,500 ft)
- **Northeast:** The Greymist Hills, shrouded in perpetual fog (1,500-2,500 ft)

---

## Major Locations

### Millbrook Village
*Population: ~800 | Government: Lord Aldric Blackwood*

The largest settlement in the region, Millbrook sits where the Silver Creek meets the Old King's Road. Originally a mill town (hence the name), it grew into a trading hub serving the surrounding farms and the now-abandoned mines.

**Key Locations:**
- **The Wandering Lantern Inn** – Three-story timber building; Marta Hearthstone, proprietor
- **Ironbelly Forge** – The best smithy for 100 miles; Gormund Ironbelly, master smith
- **Temple of the Silver Dawn** – Modest stone chapel; Father Aldous Crane, priest
- **The Garrison** – Stone watchtower and barracks; Captain Lyra Stormwind, commander
- **Blackwood Manor** – Imposing estate on the hill overlooking town; Lord Aldric Blackwood
- **The Rusty Anchor** – Disreputable dockside tavern; Zara the Fence operates from the back room
- **The Market Square** – Weekly market on Seventhday; traveling merchants like Silas Thornwood appear irregularly

**Atmosphere:** A working village with muddy streets, the smell of livestock, and honest folk trying to make a living under an unkind lord. The locals are wary of strangers but warm up quickly to those who prove helpful.

---

### The Whispering Woods
*Danger Level: Moderate | 15 miles north of Millbrook*

A vast deciduous forest stretching across the northern reaches of the region. The locals call it "Whispering" because the wind through the ancient oaks sounds like distant voices. Some say it's the spirits of those who died here; others claim the forest itself is alive.

**Notable Features:**
- **Old Mag's Cottage** – A crooked hut at the forest's southern edge where the hedge witch dwells
- **The Fairy Ring** – A perfect circle of mushrooms said to be a gateway to the fey realm (location known only to Old Mag)
- **The Hollow Oak** – An enormous dead tree, hollowed out; serves as a landmark and occasional shelter
- **Druid Stones** – Ancient standing stones deep in the forest; purpose unknown

**Inhabitants:**
- Wolves, deer, boar, and other natural wildlife
- Sprites and will-o'-wisps (especially at night)
- The occasional lost traveler who wandered off the path
- Rumors of a green dragon lairing somewhere in the depths (unconfirmed)

**Hazards:**
- Easy to get lost; the trees seem to move when you're not watching
- Will-o'-wisps lure travelers into bogs
- Wolves grow bold in winter
- The fey are capricious and may help or hinder as the mood takes them

---

### Silver Creek and the Millbrook River
*Primary water source for the region*

Silver Creek originates in the Greymist Hills and flows south through Millbrook before joining the larger Millbrook River. The river continues southwest toward the distant capital.

**Notable Features:**
- **Millbrook Falls** – A 30-foot waterfall north of town; scenic but difficult to navigate
- **The Old Mill** – Abandoned watermill on the creek; rumored cult meeting place
- **Fisherman's Bend** – Popular fishing spot; calm waters and good catch
- **The Crossing** – Stone bridge on the Old King's Road; only safe crossing for miles
- **River Cave** – A cave mouth where the creek emerges from underground (see Caves section)

**Uses:**
- Drinking water and irrigation for farms
- Fishing (trout, pike, and occasional river sprites)
- Limited barge traffic for trade goods
- The mill wheels once powered the town's industry

---

### The Greymist Hills
*Danger Level: High | 20 miles northeast of Millbrook*

A range of rugged, fog-shrouded hills that form the region's northeastern border. The mist never fully lifts, even on sunny days. Local superstition claims the hills are haunted by the ghosts of miners who died when the tunnels collapsed.

**Notable Features:**
- **The Abandoned Mines** – Network of played-out iron mines; partially flooded and unstable
- **Watcher's Peak** – Highest point in the region; an old signal tower stands at the summit
- **The Greymist Caves** – Natural cave system (see Caves section)
- **Goblin Territory** – The Skullcracker tribe lairs somewhere in these hills

**Atmosphere:** Oppressive fog, jagged rocks, and an eerie silence. Sound doesn't carry properly here—voices seem muffled while footsteps echo strangely. Locals avoid these hills after dark.

**Inhabitants:**
- Goblins of the Skullcracker tribe (hostile)
- Giant spiders in the old mine tunnels
- Bats (normal and giant varieties)
- Unconfirmed reports of "something large" moving in the mist

## Caves

### The River Cave
*Danger Level: Low to Moderate | 3 miles north of Millbrook*

Where Silver Creek emerges from underground, a cave mouth opens into the hillside. The front chambers are well-known to local children who dare each other to explore.

**Description:**
- **Entrance:** 20 feet wide, 15 feet tall; the creek flows out
- **First Chamber:** A large cavern with a sandy beach; graffiti from generations of teenagers
- **The Narrows:** A tight passage requiring wading through knee-deep water
- **Beyond:** Unexplored; locals say they hear strange echoes from deeper within

**Rumors:**
- Smugglers used to stash goods here
- A monster lives in the deep water
- The caves connect to the old mines (unconfirmed)

---

### Thornback Cave
*Danger Level: Moderate | 8 miles west of Millbrook*

A cave in a rocky outcropping, named for the thornbushes that grow thick around the entrance. Currently home to a family of brown bears—the mother is particularly aggressive.

**Description:**
- **Entrance:** Hidden by vegetation; 8 feet wide, 5 feet tall
- **Main Chamber:** 40 feet across; bear den with bones and debris
- **Back Passage:** Leads to a smaller chamber with ancient paintings on the walls
- **The Paintings:** Depict humanoid figures hunting, but also something larger with many limbs

**Dangers:**
- Mama bear (HD 4, very territorial)
- Two bear cubs (HD 1 each)
- Unstable ceiling in the back chamber

---

### The Greymist Caves
*Danger Level: High | In the Greymist Hills*

A natural cave system that predates the mines. The goblins of the Skullcracker tribe use parts of it as their lair, but even they avoid the deeper sections.

**Description:**
- **Multiple Entrances:** At least three known entrances in the hills
- **Goblin Warren:** Upper levels; crude fortifications, fire pits, prisoners
- **The Fungal Garden:** Middle levels; bioluminescent mushrooms, some hallucinogenic
- **The Deep:** Lowest levels; flooded, cold, and home to something the goblins fear

**Inhabitants:**
- 40-60 goblins of the Skullcracker tribe
- Chieftain Skullcracker (HD 3, wears a crown of bones)
- Giant bats serving as scouts and mounts
- Unknown creature(s) in the deep sections

## Ruins

### The Old Tower
*Age: ~200 years | 6 miles northeast of Millbrook*

A crumbling stone tower on a hilltop, visible from the town on clear days. Once a wizard's residence, it's been abandoned for generations after its owner "disappeared under mysterious circumstances."

**Description:**
- **Exterior:** 60 feet tall, three stories; top floor collapsed
- **Ground Floor:** Entry hall with faded murals; stairs down and up
- **Second Floor:** Library (books long rotted); laboratory (equipment rusted)
- **Basement:** Sealed door with arcane symbols; no one has opened it
- **Surroundings:** Overgrown garden with alchemical herbs gone wild

**Rumors:**
- The wizard's ghost still haunts the tower
- A treasure vault lies beneath the sealed door
- Strange lights appear in the windows on moonless nights
- Silas Thornwood has been seen near here

**Current State:** Occasionally explored by adventurers; mostly empty but the basement remains unexplored.

---

### The Sunken Temple
*Age: ~1,000 years | In the Whispering Woods*

Deep in the forest, ancient stone structures sink into the earth—the remains of a temple to a forgotten god. Trees grow through the cracked flagstones, and vines cover the fallen columns.

**Description:**
- **Above Ground:** Only the tops of columns and partial walls visible
- **The Sunken Nave:** 40 feet below ground level; accessible via collapsed walls
- **Altar Chamber:** Intact altar of black stone; radiates faint magic
- **Catacombs:** Burial chambers beneath the nave; sealed

**Features:**
- Ancient inscriptions in an unknown language
- Bas-reliefs depicting rituals involving fire and shadow
- The altar occasionally weeps a dark, oily liquid
- Compass needles spin wildly near the center

**Inhabitants:**
- Will-o'-wisps gather here on certain nights
- Undead may still guard the catacombs
- A cult may be attempting to restore the temple (rumor)

---

### The Fallen Keep
*Age: ~500 years | 12 miles south of Millbrook*

The ruins of a border fortress from a long-ago war. Little remains except tumbled walls and a collapsed gatehouse, but the underground levels are surprisingly intact.

**Description:**
- **Surface:** Rubble, partial walls 10-15 feet high, choked with ivy
- **The Gatehouse:** Most intact structure; stairs lead down
- **Underground:** Barracks, armory, prison cells—all long emptied
- **The Lord's Vault:** A sealed chamber behind a portcullis that won't budge

**History:** Built to defend against invaders from the south; abandoned after the war ended. Supposedly haunted by the garrison that died defending it.

**Current Use:** Bandits occasionally camp in the ruins; the underground is more dangerous.

## Roads and Travel

### The Old King's Road
The main trade route connecting Millbrook to the wider kingdom. Reasonably maintained near town but deteriorates into a rutted track further out.

- **South to the Capital:** 8 days by wagon, 5 days on horseback
- **East to the Border:** 3 days; less traveled, more dangerous
- **North into the Hills:** The road ends at the abandoned mines

### Travel Times from Millbrook

| Destination | On Foot | Mounted |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Whispering Woods (edge) | 5 hours | 2 hours |
| Old Tower | 2 hours | 45 min |
| River Cave | 1 hour | 30 min |
| Greymist Hills (edge) | 6 hours | 2.5 hours |
| Thornback Cave | 3 hours | 1 hour |
| Fallen Keep | 4 hours | 1.5 hours |

## Current Events and Rumors

### Confirmed
- Goblin sightings have increased near the eastern road
- Livestock has gone missing from farms north of town
- Lord Aldric is raising taxes again

### Rumors (May or may not be true)
- A cult meets in the Old Mill at midnight on the new moon
- The merchant Silas Thornwood seeks something in the Old Tower
- Strange lights have been seen in the Greymist Hills
- Old Mag has foreseen a disaster but won't speak of it
- Something in the Greymist Caves has the goblins scared
- Sir Roderick Ashford was once a knight of the realm

### Adventure Hooks
1. **The Missing Livestock:** What's taking the animals? Wolves? Goblins? Something worse?
2. **The Sealed Basement:** What lies beneath the Old Tower? Can the seal be broken?
3. **The Cult in the Mill:** Are the rumors true? What are they summoning?
4. **Goblin Troubles:** The Skullcracker tribe grows bold. Someone must deal with them.
5. **The Sunken Temple:** What god was worshipped here? Why does the altar weep?
6. **Lord Aldric's Secret:** The cruel noble is hiding something. What is it?

## DM Notes

### Tone
This is a low-magic, grounded fantasy setting with room to grow darker or more fantastical as needed. The NPCs have interconnected relationships and secrets that can drive drama even without monster encounters.

### Scaling
- **Levels 1-3:** Goblin scouts, wolves, bandits, minor undead
- **Levels 4-6:** Goblin lair, Old Tower basement, Sunken Temple upper levels
- **Levels 7-10:** Whatever lurks in the Greymist Caves depths, the cult's endgame

### Connections
Many NPCs reference each other:
- Lord Aldric is connected to the goblins (Whisper knows)
- The cult may be connected to Lord Aldric or the Sunken Temple
- Sir Roderick's past connects to court intrigue beyond the region
- Old Mag knows more than she reveals about nearly everything
