## Appearance

Nix is a scrappy, underfed child with a mop of dirty brown hair, bright green eyes too clever for their age, and a gap-toothed grin. Their clothes are ragged but functional—stolen from clotheslines and cut down to fit. They're always barefoot ("shoes slow you down"), and their hands are quick as lightning. A rope serves as a belt, holding various useful items: a sling, a whistle, a lock pick made from a bent nail.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Cocky and irreverent
- Fiercely loyal to other street kids
- Hungry for adventure and recognition
- Older than their years in some ways, childish in others

**Ideals:** Look out for your own. The rich can afford to lose a few coins.

**Bonds:** The other Street Rats are their family.

**Flaws:** Reckless bravery; doesn't know when they're outmatched.

---

## Backstory

Nix doesn't remember their parents. Their earliest memories are of the orphanage, which they fled at age six after the caretaker got too free with his fists. They've survived on the streets ever since, joining Zara's network of "Street Rats" who gather information in exchange for food and protection.

Nix dreams of being an adventurer—a real hero like in the stories. They practice with their sling, watch the town guards train, and memorize everything adventurers say in the tavern. They're convinced they're destined for greatness. They just need someone to give them a chance.

---

## Motivations

- Become a real adventurer
- Protect the younger Street Rats
- Find out who their parents were
- Prove they're more than just a thief

---

## Secrets

- They witnessed a murder near the docks and are afraid to tell anyone
- They've hidden a small treasure (20 gp) stolen from Lord Aldric's courier
- They can read—taught themselves from stolen books
- They're absolutely terrified of the dark

---

## What They Can Offer

- Guide to the streets, alleys, and rooftops
- Information from the Street Rat network
- Delivery of messages (discreet)
- Distraction services ("Look, a diversion!")
- Knows which merchants are cruel and which leave food out

---

## The Street Rats

Nix is a leader among Zara's network of orphan informants:
- **Pip:** Youngest, age 7, lookout
- **Bree:** Age 10, pickpocket, has a crush on Nix
- **Tommo:** Age 14, oldest, thinks he should be in charge
- **Squeaky:** Age 9, mute, best climber

---

## Relationships

- **Zara the Fence:** Boss and protector; Nix is secretly afraid of her
- **Marta Hearthstone:** Sneaks them scraps; pretends not to notice
- **Captain Stormwind:** Tries to "reform" them; Nix escapes every time
- **Sir Roderick:** Watched him fight off muggers once; hero worship

---

## Roleplaying Notes

- Speaks in slang and dropped letters ("'Ello, guv'ner")
- Always looking for escape routes
- Will steal anything not nailed down (and some things that are)
- Brags about adventures they haven't had yet
- Gets serious and quiet when other kids are threatened
- Desperately wants approval from "real" adventurers
- Will absolutely adopt any player character who shows them kindness
