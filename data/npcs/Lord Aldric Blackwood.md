## Appearance

Aldric is a lean, sharp-featured man with cold gray eyes and thinning black hair slicked back from a high forehead. He dresses in expensive but conservative clothing—always black and gray—and carries a silver-topped cane he doesn't need. His thin lips seem permanently fixed in a sneer, and his manicured hands have never seen a day of labor.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Condescending to everyone beneath his station (which is everyone)
- Enjoys the suffering of others
- Calculating and patient in his cruelty
- Obsessed with status and legacy

**Ideals:** Power is the only virtue. The strong rule; the weak serve.

**Bonds:** The Blackwood name must endure and grow in power.

**Flaws:** His arrogance blinds him to threats from "lesser" people.

---

## Backstory

Aldric inherited his title and lands from his father, a decent man who died under "mysterious circumstances" when Aldric was twenty. Since then, he's tripled the family fortune through ruthless taxation, strategic marriages, and eliminating rivals.

He views the peasants as livestock—useful for generating wealth but deserving of no consideration. Those who displease him disappear. Those who please him are used until they're no longer useful.

He's currently angling for a position at the royal court, which requires demonstrating both wealth and capability. The increased goblin activity is an annoyance—bad for taxation—but also an opportunity to appear heroic if he can claim credit for solving the problem.

---

## Motivations

- Gain a seat at the royal court
- Expand his territory by acquiring neighboring lands
- Produce an heir (his wife has failed to conceive)
- Crush any hint of rebellion among the peasants

---

## Secrets

- He murdered his father with slow poison
- He's embezzling taxes meant for the crown
- He's in contact with a cult seeking ancient artifacts
- His wife knows what he did and secretly hates him

---

## Relationships

- **Lady Blackwood (wife):** A political marriage; she despises him but fears him more
- **Captain Stormwind:** Useful tool; he suspects she'd kill him if she could
- **Father Aldous:** A nuisance with his "charity" and "compassion"
- **The Merchant Guild:** They pay him bribes; he protects their interests

---

## How He Can Be Used

- **Antagonist:** The obvious villain oppressing the village
- **Employer:** Hires adventurers for dirty work he can't be connected to
- **Obstacle:** Blocks investigations, protects criminals who pay him
- **Target:** Someone wants him dead (many someones, actually)

---

## Resources

- 20+ guards and men-at-arms
- Significant wealth
- Political connections
- Legal authority over the region
- A network of informants and spies

---

## Roleplaying Notes

- Never raises his voice (quiet menace is more effective)
- Addresses commoners by occupation, never by name ("innkeeper," "smith")
- Smiles only when delivering bad news
- Makes veiled threats disguised as "observations"
- Never does anything himself that a servant could do
