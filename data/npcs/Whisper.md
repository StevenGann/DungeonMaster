## Appearance

Whisper is small even for a goblin, barely three feet tall with mottled green-gray skin and oversized ears that twitch at every sound. His yellow eyes are too large for his face, giving him a permanently startled expression. He wears a patchwork cloak made of stolen rags and carries a bag full of "treasures" (mostly junk). One of his ears has a notch torn out of it—a mark of exile.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Nervous and twitchy, always looking for escape routes
- Surprisingly clever and observant
- Speaks in third person ("Whisper knows things...")
- Loyal to those who treat him kindly (a rare experience)

**Ideals:** Survival first. Friends second. Everything else doesn't matter.

**Bonds:** The "soft ones" (humans who've helped him) confuse but intrigue him.

**Flaws:** He's a coward and will flee at the first sign of real danger.

---

## Backstory

Whisper was exiled from his tribe for refusing to participate in a raid on a human village. In goblin society, this is the ultimate shame—he was marked with the ear-notch and driven out. He's survived alone for two years, scavenging from human settlements and learning their language by listening from the shadows.

He discovered that humans will pay for information about goblin movements. Now he sells what he knows to whoever will listen—and not kill him on sight. He's terrified of his former tribe finding him but equally terrified of being alone.

---

## Motivations

- Survive another day
- Find somewhere safe to belong
- Prove he's useful (so he won't be discarded)
- Get revenge on Chieftain Skullcracker who exiled him

---

## Secrets

- He knows the location of his old tribe's lair
- He's seen something "big and dark" moving in the Greymist Hills
- He witnessed Lord Aldric's men meeting with goblin raiders
- He's slowly becoming more "human" in his thinking and hates himself for it

---

## What He Knows

- Goblin tribe locations, numbers, and habits
- Secret passages through the town's sewers
- Which buildings are unoccupied and when
- Patrol schedules (he watches everything)
- Where the "shinies" (treasure) might be hidden

---

## How To Find Him

Whisper doesn't approach directly. Those seeking him should:
1. Leave food (preferably meat) near the old well at midnight
2. Say "Whisper knows" three times
3. Wait—he'll find you if he decides you're not a threat
