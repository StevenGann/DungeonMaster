## Appearance

Zara has deep crimson skin, small curved horns that sweep back over her dark hair, and eyes like molten gold. She dresses in fine clothes—always black—and wears enough jewelry to make a noblewoman jealous (most of it stolen, then "legitimized"). A thin tail curls around her ankle when she's thinking. She smells faintly of expensive perfume and pipe smoke.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Calculating and always thinking three moves ahead
- Charming when it serves her, cold when it doesn't
- Never shows surprise
- Treats loyalty as a transaction

**Ideals:** Trust is for fools. Information is the only currency that matters.

**Bonds:** She protects the street children who serve as her eyes and ears.

**Flaws:** She's made enemies of powerful people and lives in constant fear of discovery.

---

## Backstory

Zara grew up on the streets, an orphan tiefling shunned for her appearance. She learned early that information was more valuable than gold—knowing who was sleeping with whom, who owed money to whom, who was planning what. By twenty, she had built a network of informants. By thirty, she controlled half the black market in the dockside district.

She operates out of The Rusty Anchor tavern, conducting business in a back room accessible only to those who know the password (which changes weekly).

---

## Motivations

- Maintain her power and independence
- Accumulate enough wealth to retire somewhere far away
- Destroy the Thieves' Guild that betrayed her years ago
- Protect "her kids"—the street urchins who work for her

---

## Secrets

- She's secretly feeding information to the city watch—for a price
- She knows the identity of the masked noble who runs the slave trade
- She keeps blackmail material on half the city's merchants
- She has a hidden escape route out of the city

## Roleplaying Notes

- Speaks in a low, measured voice
- Never sits with her back to the door
- Taps her claws on the table when impatient
