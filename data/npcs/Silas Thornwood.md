## Appearance

Silas is tall and gaunt with angular features that betray his elven heritage. His dark hair is streaked with silver and tied back in a ponytail. He wears a long coat of deep purple velvet, slightly worn at the elbows, and carries a gnarled walking stick topped with a carved raven. His wagon is painted black with silver stars and pulled by two mismatched horses.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Speaks in riddles and half-truths
- Never gives a straight answer if he can avoid it
- Always seems to know more than he should
- Treats every transaction as a game

**Ideals:** Everything has a price. The trick is knowing what it's worth.

**Bonds:** His wagon contains his entire life—he'd die before losing it.

**Flaws:** His curiosity often leads him into dangerous situations.

---

## Backstory

Silas claims to have been a merchant for "longer than most kingdoms have existed." No one knows where he came from or where he goes between visits. He appears at seemingly random times, often when his wares are most needed. Some say he's blessed by a god of travelers; others whisper he made a deal with something darker.

He trades in the unusual: rare spell components, maps to forgotten places, cursed items he "forgot to mention" were cursed, and occasionally, information.

---

## Motivations

- Collect rare and unusual items
- Witness interesting events (he's easily bored)
- Find a specific item he's been searching for over a century (he won't say what)

---

## Secrets

- He can sense magical items and is drawn to them
- He owes a debt to a powerful fey lord
- His wagon is larger on the inside than the outside
- He's seen the rise and fall of three empires
