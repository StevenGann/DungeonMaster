## Appearance

Mag is a bent, wizened woman with wild gray hair that seems to have a mind of its own. Her eyes are different colors—one brown, one milky white—and her nose is hooked like a bird's beak. She wears layers of patched shawls and skirts in earthy colors, hung with pouches of herbs, bones, and unidentifiable charms. She smells of woodsmoke, rosemary, and something faintly unpleasant.

---

## Backstory

Mag was once a midwife and healer in the village, but as she aged and grew stranger, the villagers grew fearful. When a child she couldn't save died, they drove her out with stones and accusations of witchcraft. She's lived in her crooked cottage for thirty years, tending to those desperate enough to seek her out.

She *does* practice magic—but it's hedge magic, the old folk magic of herbs and spirits, not the dark arts. She can speak to animals, predict weather, and brew potions that actually work. Father Aldous believes she's touched by something supernatural but not necessarily evil.

## Motivations
