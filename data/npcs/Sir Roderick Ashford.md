## Appearance

Roderick was once a handsome man, and traces of it remain beneath the ruin. He has a weathered face, a broken nose that healed crooked, and haunted blue eyes. His once-proud bearing has slumped into the posture of a man defeated. He still wears his old armor—dented and poorly maintained—and keeps his family sword, though the crest has been scratched off the pommel.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Bitter and self-pitying when drunk
- Glimpses of his former nobility when sober
- Protective of the innocent, despite everything
- Self-destructive; seeks death but can't bring himself to find it

**Ideals:** (Once) A knight protects the weak and upholds justice. (Now) Nothing matters anymore.

**Bonds:** The memory of who he used to be haunts him.

**Flaws:** He drinks to forget and has forgotten who he is.

---

## Backstory

Roderick was a knight of the Silver Order, sworn to protect the innocent and uphold justice. Five years ago, he failed in his duty. During an ambush, he froze—and the young prince he was guarding was killed. The official story blamed bandits, but Roderick knew the truth: his moment of cowardice cost a child's life.

He was stripped of his title and banished. Rather than face his shame, he's spent five years wandering from tavern to tavern, taking mercenary work when he needs coin for drink, and slowly destroying himself.

But somewhere, buried deep beneath the guilt and alcohol, the knight he was still exists. He just needs a reason to remember.

---

## Motivations

- Drink enough to stop seeing the prince's face
- Find something worth dying for
- Secretly hopes someone will force him to be better
- Fears redemption because he doesn't deserve it

---

## Secrets

- The ambush that killed the prince was arranged by someone at court
- He knows who ordered it but has no proof
- He has a hidden cache of his knight's regalia, including a holy symbol
- He's received messages offering to restore his honor—for a price

---

## What He Can Offer

- Expert combat training (+2 to Physical for students)
- Knowledge of noble houses, politics, and military tactics
- A skilled blade for hire (when sober)
- Connections (burned bridges, but bridges nonetheless)
- The potential to become a loyal ally if redeemed

---

## Redemption Arc (Optional)

Roderick can become a valuable ally if players:
1. Discover the truth about the prince's death
2. Give him a cause worth fighting for
3. Help him confront his guilt
4. Challenge him to be the man he used to be

When redeemed: +2 to all stats, sobriety, and unwavering loyalty.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Quotes the Knight's Code sarcastically
- Orders "whatever's strongest"
- Flinches when called "Sir"
- Occasionally displays startling competence
- Speaks formally when sober, slurs when drunk
- Stares into his drink for long periods
- Will intervene if he sees someone being bullied (then hate himself for caring)
