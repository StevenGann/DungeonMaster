## Appearance

Gormund is a barrel-chested dwarf with a magnificent braided beard the color of iron ore, streaked with silver. His arms are covered in old burn scars—badges of honor from decades at the forge. He wears a leather apron over simple clothes, and his hands are permanently stained with soot. One of his front teeth is gold (replaced after a "disagreement" with a customer).

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Gruff and slow to warm up
- Takes immense pride in his work
- Surprisingly patient with apprentices
- Hates shoddy craftsmanship

**Ideals:** A weapon should outlast the smith who made it.

**Bonds:** His forge is his legacy; his reputation is everything.

**Flaws:** He's stubborn to a fault and holds grudges for decades.

---

## Backstory

Gormund left the dwarven holds after a dispute with his clan over modernizing forging techniques. "They wanted to do things the old way just because it was old," he grumbles. He wandered for fifty years before settling in Millbrook, drawn by the quality of local iron ore.

He's been the village smith for nearly a century, outworking and outlasting three generations of human competitors. The noble families of three kingdoms have standing orders for his weapons, though he only takes commissions that "interest" him.

---

## Motivations

- Create a masterwork weapon that will be remembered for centuries
- Find a worthy apprentice to pass on his techniques
- Acquire rare metals (adamantine, mithril)
- Never return to the dwarven holds (though he misses them)

---

## Secrets

- He knows the formula for a nearly-lost dwarven steel alloy
- He once forged a weapon for a demon (unknowingly—it haunts him)
- He has a hidden vault of his finest work that he refuses to sell
- He's dying of a slow lung sickness from decades of forge smoke
