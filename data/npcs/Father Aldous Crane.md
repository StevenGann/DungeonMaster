## Appearance

Father Aldous is a frail, elderly man with a kind face deeply lined by age and worry. His white hair forms a wispy halo around his balding head, and his pale blue eyes still hold a gentle spark. He walks with a slight limp (old injury) and always carries a worn prayer book. His silver holy symbol hangs from a simple cord around his neck.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Gentle and patient with everyone
- Sees the good in people, sometimes naively
- Scholarly; loves ancient texts
- Quietly stubborn about his principles

**Ideals:** Every soul can be redeemed. Compassion is the highest virtue.

**Bonds:** His congregation and the temple are his entire world.

**Flaws:** He's too trusting and often taken advantage of.

---

## Backstory

Aldous has served the Temple of the Silver Dawn for over forty years. He came to Millbrook as a young acolyte and never left. He's baptized, married, and buried three generations of villagers. The temple is modest—barely more than a stone chapel—but to Aldous, it's the heart of the community.

In his youth, he was briefly an adventurer, traveling with a group of heroes who defeated a minor demon lord. He rarely speaks of those days, but occasionally old companions send letters or visit.

---

## Motivations

- Care for his flock's spiritual and physical needs
- Restore the temple's crumbling roof before winter
- Train a successor (he's getting old)
- Protect the sacred relic hidden beneath the temple

---

## Secrets

- Beneath the temple is a sealed chamber containing a dangerous artifact
- He's having a crisis of faith; his prayers feel hollow lately
- He knows the true identity of a villager hiding from a dark past
- Years ago, he failed to save someone, and the guilt still haunts him
