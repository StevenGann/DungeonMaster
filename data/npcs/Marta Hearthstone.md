## Appearance

Marta is a stout, broad-shouldered woman with kind brown eyes and silver-streaked auburn hair usually kept in a practical bun. Her face is weathered but warm, with deep laugh lines around her eyes. She wears a flour-dusted apron over simple homespun clothes and always has a wooden spoon tucked into her belt.

## Personality

**Traits:**
- Warm and motherly to travelers
- Sharp-tongued with troublemakers
- Never forgets a face or a name
- Protective of her regulars

**Ideals:** Everyone deserves a warm meal and a safe place to rest.

**Bonds:** The Wandering Lantern has been in her family for three generations.

**Flaws:** She gossips too freely when she's had a few drinks.

---

## Backstory

Marta inherited The Wandering Lantern from her mother, who inherited it from *her* mother. She's seen countless adventurers pass through—some became legends, others were never heard from again. Her husband Tomas died of fever ten years ago, and she's raised her two children (now grown) on her own since then.

She knows every regular in the village and most of the traveling merchants who pass through. If there's news to be had, Marta has heard it. If there's a rumor circulating, she probably started it.

---

## Motivations

- Keep her inn prosperous and her customers safe
- Eventually pass the inn to her daughter Eliza
- Pay off the debt she owes to the merchant guild

---

## Secrets

- She hides runaway servants in her cellar when the lord's men come looking
- Her late husband was once an adventurer; his old sword is hidden under the floorboards
- She waters down the ale for customers she doesn't like

---

## Relationships

- **Eliza (daughter):** Works as a barmaid, headstrong and wants to leave the village
- **Bram (son):** Apprentice blacksmith, visits every week
- **Lord Aldric:** The local noble; Marta despises his cruelty but pays her taxes
- **Old Willem:** The village drunk; Marta lets him sleep in the stable

---

## Useful Information She Knows

- Which roads are safe and which have seen bandit activity
- Rumors about the abandoned tower in the Greymist Hills
- Who in town can be trusted and who cannot
- Where to find the best hunting grounds

---

## Roleplaying Notes

- Addresses everyone as "dear" or "love"
- Speaks with a slight country accent
- Will offer food before asking questions
- Becomes guarded if asked about her husband's past
